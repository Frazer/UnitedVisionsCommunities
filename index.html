The Dream

Imagine hundreds of people living and building together — connected by archways and gardens, sharing meals, sharing creativity, sharing a commitment to transform their minds, their relationships, and the world. This is not a retreat. This is how we want to live.

Six Pillars

The Foundations of Our Way of Life

01

Mindful Living

Morning meditations, community bells, and collective mental rehearsal to start every day with intention.

"The whole community comes in excited to level up their mind."

Each day begins with a shared meditation — not a quiet, solitary act, but a collective experience with epic bells, dramatic lighting, and the energy of hundreds of people orienting themselves toward their highest potential.

Compassion meditation is a daily practice. You move through the community and feel genuinely loved, cared for, and supported — not by chance, but because everyone has cultivated that quality of presence together.

Mental rehearsal and positive visualization are woven into everyday rhythms. We rehearse who we want to be, not just what we want to accomplish.

02

Communal Connection

Shared meals, cooperative living, and loving relationships — the simple infrastructure of belonging.

"You're on the roster once every couple of weeks — you make something great, share it with everybody, and every other meal is shared with you."

Communal living radically simplifies the logistics of daily life. Freed from cooking three meals a day alone, you have energy and attention for what matters most. The shared kitchen becomes a place of creativity and generosity.

Beautiful architecture with archways and hanging gardens connects building to building. The physical environment is intentionally designed so chance encounters are beautiful, and solitude is always available when needed.

Gyms, sports grounds, a movie theater — the amenities of a rich life, shared rather than individually owned. Community is not a compromise. It's an upgrade.

03

Creative Culture

Fashion lines, VR worlds, films, books, podcasts — and the legendary "Masaya Jams" where creativity meets enlightenment.

"Imagine you spending some time just role-playing being as expressive and as enlightening as you can."

Masaya Jams are live community sessions where cameras roll and everyone is encouraged to inhabit the kindest, most awesome, wildest version of themselves — like theater games, but the goal is enlightenment. The output is something worth sharing with the world.

The community actively creates: fashion lines where the imagery makes people feel love for all beings; VR worlds built collaboratively in an afternoon; films, TV shows, books, and podcasts carrying a clear intention toward human flourishing.

Music, art, and the words in our spaces are chosen intentionally. Lyrics that lift. Visuals that inspire. The culture we make together can be exported everywhere.

04

Ethics & Impact

Ethical consumption, environmental care, and a shared commitment to not causing harm — in every purchase, every choice.

"We don't buy stuff from places where anyone was hurt — and we're kind to the environment."

Ethics isn't a policy here — it's a shared value running through every decision. Where we source food. What we build with. How we engage the wider world. The community has agreed that its positive intention extends not just to each other, but to all living beings.

Every member is excited not just to help one another, but to have a positive impact beyond these walls. We want everything we make, buy, eat, and create to be a net positive in the world.

This is not austerity. Ethical living is joyful, beautiful, and abundant. It simply requires intention — and a community committed to holding each other to it, with kindness.

05

Education & Growth

Schools where children learn mindfulness and confidence — not just academic preparation.

"A culture where people are actively helping everyone's mind be awesome is a very different culture."

Our schools teach mindfulness, kindness, emotional confidence, and how to handle different scenarios with grace. Children mentally rehearse being their best selves — the same practice the adults use, taught from the very beginning.

Consider the difference: in a school where no one teaches mental rehearsal, some children will naturally develop it and others will rehearse failure. We choose to deliberately bring every mind toward its highest potential.

This extends beyond school: workplaces where teams practice positive visualization together, where friendships are loving and genuinely supportive. The whole environment is designed for flourishing.

06

World Outreach

Welcoming visitors, inspiring world leaders, and sharing the culture we've built with everyone ready to receive it.

"How do we inspire filmmakers, sports stars, and world leaders to come practice meditation with us — and feel what it's like to envision a world where everybody cares?"

United Visions is not a refuge from the world — it's a laboratory for it. Visitors come to experience the architecture, the food, the gardens, the art, and the rare feeling of being in a community truly oriented toward human flourishing.

We actively look for ways to share what works. Creative culture, spiritual practices, ethical frameworks — the Masaya Jams are streamed, the films are released, the books are published.

We encourage everyone to find or create their own intentional communities: to explore what resonates, practice their dreams, and discover that society itself becomes your commune when you approach it with real intention.
